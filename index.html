Link your Excel file

Choose the tracking list that lives in your OneDrive folder on this computer. Changes you make here are saved straight into that file, and OneDrive syncs it to Excel online.

Good to know
  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a laptop or desktop. On a phone the page can’t reach the file, so use the backup buttons below there.
  2. Pick the file inside your OneDrive folder (File Explorer → OneDrive), not a copy from Downloads.
  3. Your browser asks for permission to edit the file. On later visits you may need to click Reconnect Excel once.
  4. Close the file in desktop Excel while you edit here, and avoid editing in both places at the same moment.
  5. Cell colours stay where they are in the sheet. If you add rows here, tidy the styling in Excel. OneDrive’s version history lets you roll back anytime.
